# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good faith. Remember that the person on the other side is probably
tired, probably learning, and probably doing this in their spare time.

That covers about 99% of it. The rest is detail.

## What's expected

- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone was one. This project was itself built by
  someone learning as they went, and it shows in places.
- **Critique the code, not the person.** "This will double-post under X" is useful.
  "This is terrible" is not.
- **Accept that people have less time than you'd like.** Reviews may take days.
  A nudge after a week is fine; five nudges in two days is not.
- **Say when you don't know.** "I'm not sure this is right, but…" is a strength here.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discrimination or derogatory comments relating to gender, gender identity, sexual
  orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age,
  religion, nationality, or caste
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing threads

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere
else people interact around this project.

## Reporting

If someone is making this project worse to be part of, email **ys9410017064@gmail.com**.

Reports are read privately. You will not be asked to justify why something bothered
you, and reporting in good faith will never count against you — even if the outcome
is that nothing needs to change.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block
accounts, depending on what's warranted. Where possible, a quiet word comes first;
where it isn't, it doesn't.
